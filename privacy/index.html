SMS / Text Messaging Policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

When you provide your mobile phone number and consent to receive text messages from Sturm & Róisín, LLC or any of its ventures, you agree to the following terms:

  • Program description: You may receive SMS messages related to appointment reminders, order updates, service notifications, account alerts, and occasional promotional communications.
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 10 messages per month, depending on your interactions and the services you use.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier's standard messaging and data rates apply to all SMS messages sent and received.
  • Opt-out: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.
  • Help: For assistance, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at sturmroisin@gmail.com.
  • Consent: Consent to receive text messages is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We use Twilio as our messaging service provider. Your phone number and opt-in data are shared with Twilio solely for the purpose of delivering text messages on our behalf. Twilio's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy.

Mobile opt-in data — including your phone number and consent status — will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes at any time.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:
